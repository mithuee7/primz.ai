Privacy Policy

Last updated September 2026

This policy explains what information Primz collects through this website, how it is used, and how it is stored. It applies to visitors of this site and anyone who submits a request through our forms.

Information we collect

When you submit a custom request form, we collect the name, contact details (email or phone), and description of needs that you choose to provide. We do not collect payment information through this site.

How we use it

Information submitted through our forms is used only to respond to your request, understand what you need, and follow up with you about our services. We do not use this information for advertising or share it with advertisers.

Where it is stored

Submitted requests are stored in a Supabase database and processed through a server hosted on Render. Access to this data is restricted to the founders of Primz through a password protected internal panel.

Cookies

This site uses a single session cookie, only when logging into the internal admin panel, to keep that session authenticated. We do not use tracking or advertising cookies on this site.

Sharing of information

We do not sell or rent your information. It is only accessible to Primz and the infrastructure providers necessary to run this site (Supabase and Render), who process it solely to provide their hosting and database services to us.

Your rights

You can request that we delete or update any information you have submitted to us by contacting us directly.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our services change. Continued use of this site after changes means you accept the updated policy.
